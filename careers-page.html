Careers Page

Built for a creator marketplace scaling fast, ahead of a platform pivot

Role
Product Designer

Platform
Responsive web, designed in Figma
  1. 01Problem
  2. 02Approach
  3. 03Solution
  4. 04Results
01Problem

A company mid-pivot, hiring the team to get it there

StarBasis is a name changed here for confidentiality. It ran a platform where fans booked paid experiences, calls and shout-outs with well-known people. After closing a new funding round, it was preparing to pivot into a broader platform for creators, adding payments and tools for webinars, communities and other online products.

That shift needed new people, and the careers page was where they would meet the company first. It had to present a business in the middle of changing, make its culture legible, and reach candidates on whatever device they happened to open it on.

02Approach

Study how others show a culture, then settle the structure before the surface

I took the page through a full process in Figma, settling the structure at low fidelity before styling any of it.

  1. 01

    Research

    How other companies in the space present their culture, their team and their benefits.

  2. 02

    Sketches

    Different ways to show the team and the benefits, explored before committing to one.

  3. 03

    Wireframe

    The structure settled at low fidelity: hero, benefits, team and open positions.

  4. 04

    Validation

    A round of validation on the structure, before the final pass.

  5. 05

    Final UI

    Every section at every breakpoint, fully responsive.

03Solution

Four sections, each doing one job

I delivered the Figma file, covering every section at every breakpoint. Beyond the original brief, I also built a coded interactive version with Claude.

Hero

Job cards drift with the cursor while a soft capsule shape breathes behind them.

Benefits

One clear reason to join, framed inside a ringed portrait that reuses the hero's shape language.

Team

The whole team, orbiting. Every face rides its own ring at its own speed.

Open Positions

A real, filterable list. Search by title or narrow by department.

04Results

The page did the job it was built for

The roles were filled, and the new product reached a full release.

The company hired the people it needed for the pivot, and the broader creator platform went on to launch in full.
