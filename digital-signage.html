Overview

The interfaces were developed to fit any screen with any aspect ratio, including landscape, portrait, bar, or square, so responsiveness was crucial to every one of these projects. Apps, as they're called on the OnSign platform, are a powerful way to present dynamic content on screens.

More than 40 interfaces were designed for the most variable content, including weather, news, exchange rates, menu boards, and many others. All of them 100% responsive.
