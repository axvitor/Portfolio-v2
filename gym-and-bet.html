Gym&Bet

Betting with friends to make fitness fun

Role
Product Designer
Scope
Wireframes to final UI, mobile app
Timeline
2 weeks
Platform
Mobile app · iOS

Overview

Gym&Bet is a mobile app for monitoring health and fitness progress, with social betting to encourage exercise participation.

Regular exercise is crucial for maintaining both physical and mental wellbeing. This is where betting with friends can make a significant difference. By turning exercise into a social activity and incorporating an element of friendly competition, it becomes more dynamic and enjoyable.

Scope

The design project took 2 weeks to complete, focusing solely on essential features to deliver value to the user before being implemented by the engineering team.
